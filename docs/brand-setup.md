# Brand setup for your team

**Lionstone is free and open source, and everything in this guide works without paying anyone.**
You can do all of it yourself with [the Reels guide](reels.md).

Some brands and agencies would rather have it set up for them. **Kreatif Hizmetler** (Creative Services) offers that as an optional service,
by quote: you fill in a short form and we get back to you.

## What's included

| Step | What you get |
|---|---|
| Installation | Lionstone, FFmpeg and the Claude skill working on your team's computers |
| Brand profile | `brand.json` and `brand.md`: audience, tone, products, honest promises, banned phrases, CTA, colours, font |
| Brand identity | Measured from your talking-head take and 3-5 example Reels, reviewed with you |
| Catalogue choices | The hook title, captions, colour, pace, effects, sound and covers that fit, chosen with you from the visual catalogue |
| First Reel | One Reel taken from your footage to a checked delivery, so your team sees the whole flow |
| Handover | Your team runs it alone afterwards. All files stay yours and on your computers |

## What doesn't change

- The software stays free under Apache-2.0. The service is for setup work, not for a license: nothing is locked
  and no feature is held back.
- There's no account, no upload and no subscription in Lionstone. Your footage stays on your computers.
- No one can promise a viral video, and neither do we.
